# OpenTT3D development .43 — shore studies and conservative HQ integration

This is a **development prerelease**, not an8/10 model-quality or sustained60fps
milestone. [Immutable `.42`](https://github.com/cstanislav/opentt3d/releases/tag/opentt3d-dev-20261003.42)
remains the recommended playable checkpoint. Existing releases and saves are not
replaced. The catalogue-wide goal remains unfinished.

## Included work

- Twenty new manually authored sea-level shore-bank volumes, individually screened
  ten at4/10 and ten at5/10. All120 retained bank studies stay **unbound**; no live
  bank replacement or accepted bank runtime coverage is claimed.
- Five public terrain-type surveys with command-correlated acknowledgements,
  original saved tile/slope/height preservation and complete source/wave evidence.
  Snow and rainforest are observed; complete desert/parameter/grid/grass/snowline
  coverage remains open. Failed/interleaved controls are preserved, not counted.
- All84 previously quarantined larger-HQ source owners and their exact provisional
  reviews are integrated conservatively. Proposed bindings are retained in an
  explicitly diagnostic sidecar, not activated in the playable runtime; original
  complete-family fallback remains intact. Original working bytes are archived.
- The explicit ownership-plane union compiler operation and its regression tests
  preserve existing cells, registration and all six face colours.

## Acceptance limits

The complete raw shore source/native audit still fails20 images/4,065 RGBA pixels;
complete paired model galleries fail35 views/165 pixels. No cropping, masking,
resizing or tolerance hides these failures. Quality gates and the exact original
wave/bank decomposition remain unaccepted. The playable1,915-model catalogue has
2,950 quality rows/141 individual reviews/four broad gaps/zero approvals; the larger
2,035-model bank diagnostic scope has3,070 rows/261 individual reviews/zero approvals.
Required-eight fails in both scopes.

Doubled-terrain bank support, every source/state/conditional/LOD/picking/ship axis,
remaining effects/fleet clearances, larger-HQ natural upgrades, strict whole-world
renderer equality, sustained60fps/memory and platform/input/replay/network
acceptance remain open. Windows ARM64 is a packaged cross-build, not an executed
Windows ARM64 acceptance test. Windows GPU/foreground input is not inferred from
archive checks. No default simulation, command/save format or simulation RNG
change is intended. Object dimensions, doubled rendered terrain,40-degree lens,
unlimited draw distance and no Cab zoom remain intact.

Downloads are attached only after the original eight-job packaging workflow and
independent hosted checksum/source/resource/native-runtime checks pass. Per-platform
JSON reports describe precisely which package tests executed; packaging success
is not whole-catalogue visual, performance or platform acceptance.
