# River rocks and Toyland islands — eight unbound 3D studies

Four original Classic slope arrangements now have explicit angular rock volumes;
four Toyland arrangements have independent yellow/pale island volumes with green
top-face paint. These are **diagnostic studies, not canonical runtime coverage**.
The source-datum models use original tile units and anchors. Independent animated
water, banks and foam are not extruded into solid terrain or bundled into relief.
Additional doubled-terrain support still needs a separate exact implementation.

All eight first studies and failures are retained. After individual source/native/
orbit/street inspection, a second pass adds fuller rock faces and the SW central
return, and corrects the islands' skewed/thin caps and cliff profiles. All eight
fingerprints change; fresh reviews are **5/10 each**, with explicit unresolved
source/profile/material/world/state defects and all six acceptance gates false.
No model or mapped source instance earns 8/10 or inherits another's approval.

## Evidence and retained failures

`initial-authored-source.json`, both frozen catalogues/compiler snapshots, both
sets of individual sheets, all native runs and all strict failures are retained.
384 portable PNGs reconstruct 443,307,448 exact PAM bytes, including full model/atlas
controls; sixteen full lossless worlds and original saved controls remain available.
All original `build-macos/` PAMs, sources, rejected studies and captures remain
untouched. Portable image compaction copies only; it deletes no original. Native
unit-runner stdout is also retained byte-for-byte in a checked lossless gzip archive,
including its original trailing blank line.

`separate-source-paint.py` is a manually annotated **paint study**, never a geometry
generator or a renderer comparison mask. Its first counter mistakenly divided
already-native PAM pixels by the sprite-cache zoom. The assertion, script and
partial output remain under `source-paint-separation*` and
`retained-source-paint-counter-failure.py`; corrected normalization uses actual
portable image dimensions, not the cached texture's metadata. The earlier
`all-eight-relief-sources.png` caption says display 8x although the retained display
is 5x; corrected source inspection grids and review sheets use their stated scales.

Use fresh prefixes/directories; the drivers refuse overwrites:


the Pillow art interpreter. `verify-portable.py --seal` refreshes evidence hashes,
not a stopping clock or acceptance. No runtime source, canonical binding, package,

ownership/phases, banks/custom states/ships, lock/HQ repairs, remaining families,
