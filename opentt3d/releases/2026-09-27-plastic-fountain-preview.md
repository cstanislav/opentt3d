# Plastic fountains with paired eight-state animation

This preview adds **16 provisional plastic-fountain volumes**, bringing the catalogue
to **1,617** and primary industry coverage to **150/175 definitions**.

- Preserve eight rounded-droplet/tapered-jet splash poses and eight independently
  animated recessed basin grounds, with white/yellow retaining edges and liquid lobes.
- Retain **24 genuinely empty early construction bodies** and all ground-stage aliases.
- Pass **24 background controls**, including **30 actual construction-layer selections**,
  full17/17 and empty0/17 trucks, four climates, clipping and app bundles.
- Observe every matching ground/body pair on **one unchanged live tile**, on both
  OpenGL and Vulkan. Missing bindings and paused one-pose controls correctly fail.
- Operate a real **plastic fountain → toy factory** route with accepted cargo and return.
- Verify **40 palettes**, **48 aliases**, **192 climate comparisons**, every preceding
  **1,601 model hash** and all **48 native OpenGL/Vulkan captures**.

The native suite passes **200 tests**, the asset suite **134** and the harness **12**.
All16 factored models exactly match their reviewed prototypes. Liquid crest/paint,
small beads, grains and rim detail remain provisional; final visual approvals remain
zero. Wider backend differences remain recorded. Short service runs average about60fps
but retain72/77 intervals above20ms; sustained smooth60fps remains open.
