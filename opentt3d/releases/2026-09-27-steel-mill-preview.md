# OpenTT3D development preview: steel mills

**28 provisional volumes** cover steel-mill tiles52…57 through their original
construction and completed states. Coverage reaches **1,354 volumes,76 industry
body definitions and80 independent grounds**.

- Exposed construction frames, glazed red roof bays, hollow chimneys and genuinely
  recessed furnace mouths follow the original multi-tile works.
- Lower buildings, machinery, casting channels and hot stock retain their separate
  ground-layer ownership. Tile55 keeps its original empty middle/completed body.
- Original-identical construction1/2 stages share models. A144-pair climate audit
  preserves the six distinct Toyland initial-ground layers under the existing guard.
- Original palette entries animate the molten metal. A read-only live observer
  requires seven fire-colour phases on each selected tile's emitted voxel ground.
- Registered source review fixes the steel models' vertical cell scale, boiler-roof
  axis, casting-roof footprint and lower support ownership. All45 bound palettes
  agree with the source layers, and prior model geometry/material hashes are retained.

Verification passes200 native tests,126 asset checks,9 harness checks and24 final
OpenGL/Vulkan/background app-bundle controls. Actual saves verify90 construction
layer selections and seven fire-palette phases on all six steel tiles per backend.
