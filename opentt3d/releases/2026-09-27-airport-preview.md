# Airport surfaces and elevated heliport

This preview adds **71 provisional volumes**, bringing the catalogue to **1,539**.
All **74 primary airport tile definitions** now have body or ground-only bindings,
with56 body owners and74 independent grounds. Distinct Toyland terminal/hangar paint,
exact lamp phases and catalogue-wide final fidelity remain unfinished.

- Add four-climate grass, taxiway, worn airfield and small-runway surfaces, retaining
  independent ground ownership and original empty body sequences.
- Bind every radar/windsock ground frame and the two source-identical triangular
  grass overlays without changing their original animation or climate behavior.
- Add the original stepped high-rise heliport, recessed glazing, real railing
  openings and round pad at the original **60-unit** local landing height.
- Verify all120 Guru/84 Powernaut support corners on the actual voxel deck, plus
  all original rotor states and stopped-to-running transitions. The fixture builds
  and operates the service through public gameplay commands.

Verification passes **200 native,131 asset and12 harness checks**, plus **40 background
controls** across the surface and heliport checkpoints. Existing source geometry and
paint hashes remain unchanged. Native registered captures agree across backends;
street-edge differences and source-art refinements remain retained. Final visual
approvals remain at zero, and sustained smooth60fps is still an open goal.
