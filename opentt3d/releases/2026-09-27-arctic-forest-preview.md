# Independent Arctic forest artwork and climate selection

Six provisional Arctic forest volumes bring the catalogue to **1,673 models**.
The forest now has independently authored snowy growth, felled timber and ground
layers selected through explicit climate states. All1,667 preceding model hashes
remain unchanged.

## Play

The [verified `.21` desktop release](https://github.com/cstanislav/opentt3d/releases/tag/opentt3d-dev-20260927.21)
includes this Arctic forest preview for Windows, macOS and Linux. Required graphics are bundled;
see the [installation and first-launch guide](https://github.com/cstanislav/opentt3d/blob/main/opentt3d/PLAYING.md).

## New artwork and original behavior

- Four original growth stages, each with nine separately rooted conifers, attached
  snow tiers and drooping branch tips.
- A supported six-tier timber pile, four stumps and independently owned snow/thaw
  ground, retaining original stage aliases and placement.
- Explicit `climate*16+stage` industry bindings. Original shared-source restrictions,
  independent body/ground selection and connected-source guards are preserved.
- Native galleries and live capture logs report the actual selected climate/state.
  Gameplay, harvest timing and simulation RNG remain unchanged.
