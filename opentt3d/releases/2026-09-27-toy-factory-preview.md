# Toy factories with the original working press

This preview adds **twelve provisional factory volumes**, bringing the catalogue to
**1,636** and basic primary-industry bindings to **160/175 definitions**.

- Preserve seven original construction/completed body owners, four independently
  selected children and separate checker-soil ground. Keep **fourteen empty body states**.
- Follow the original **50-frame** clay-to-duck sequence, child order and genuine
  absences. Conveyor parts move along their original world axis; the press uses its
  **twelve original heights**. Each child keeps independent culling and tile picking.
- Pass **28 frozen background controls**, including **68 construction-layer selections**,
  full/empty battery and toy trucks, four climates, clipping and development app bundles.
- Capture every original frame on one unchanged tile/industry during **two 9,000-frame
  supplied-service runs**, on OpenGL and Vulkan. Ordinary battery delivery starts production.
- Verify **38 palettes**, **39 aliases**, **144 climate comparisons** and all **1,624
  preceding model hashes**. All **300 integrated Vulkan views** match the reviewed prototype.
- Reject a missing holder binding while retaining all six independently owned grounds.

The native suite passes **201 tests**, the asset suite **136**, and the harness **12**.
Static body joins have no overlapping cells. Original source drawing fully hides the
duck beneath the press at frame30; the corresponding compression and adjacent poses
remain documented. Exact all-angle contact, surface paint and registration remain
provisional, with **zero final visual approvals**. Small native-composite and wider
backend pixel differences remain recorded. The two service runs average about60fps
but retain timing outliers; sustained smooth60fps remains open.
