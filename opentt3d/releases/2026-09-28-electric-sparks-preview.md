# Six-state voxel electric sparks

Electric locomotives now emit source-specific cyan and white voxel sparks, with connected
early branches and detached late fragments. The catalogue reaches **1,731 models**,
preserving every earlier model and binding.

## Changes

- Added five volumes representing all six original electric-spark sprite states through
  24 explicit climate bindings.
- Verified that the final two source sprites have identical pixels, offsets and palettes;
  they share artwork while retaining their distinct original animation timing.
- Preserved original spawning, stationary placement, smoke settings and simulation RNG.
- Added a read-only lifetime audit for both original effect/emitter pool orders.

## Verification

Twelve prototype, twenty integrated and two fallback controls pass, together with 206
native, 143 asset and 31 harness checks. All 600 integration images match staging;
648 original exports and 168 earlier diesel views remain exact. All six source bounds
match, and native OpenGL/Vulkan views agree.

Eight live controls capture 803 observations and 30 complete presented lifetimes.
Original reduced/disabled smoke passes. Earlier failed studies remain preserved alongside
their corrected source-registered and joined-view controls.
