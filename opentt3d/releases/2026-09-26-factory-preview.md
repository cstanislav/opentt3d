their original construction states and both multi-block layouts. The catalogue now
contains **1,301 volumes**, with **62 industry body definitions and66 grounds**.

- Separate excavations, open construction walls and finished brick halls, joined
  blue roofing, three hollow flues, glazed towers and low service sheds.
- Original ownership is preserved: completed42 has an empty body, while its whole
  service building belongs to the ground. Tower40 owns no invented adjoining roof;
  its lower dark roof remains part of its independent ground sprite.

  unchanged IDs. Soil/forest/rig grounds, rig bodies and one completed paper-mill

  the complete scene and four vehicle shards retain their existing coverage.

  a hash ledger, while original artwork, failures and selected comparison sheets
  remain available. The development app links directly to the current build.
