# Five-stage voxel train steam

Steam trains now emit source-specific voxel puffs that expand into open billows and
disperse into detached fragments. The catalogue reaches **1,720 models**, preserving
all earlier models and bindings.

## Changes

- Added five original steam frames with twenty explicit climate bindings.
- Preserved original spawning, upward motion, sprite timing, smoke settings and
  simulation RNG. Puffs retain their spawned position as the train moves away.
- Added a read-only lifetime audit and an ordinary build/sell-spare-train fixture
  to check both original effect-pool update orders.

## Verification

Twelve prototype, eighteen integrated and two fallback controls pass. The native suite
passes 206 checks, the asset suite 143 and the harness suite 25. All 592 integration
images match staging; 648 original source images remain exact. All five source bounds
match, and native OpenGL/Vulkan views agree.

Eight live replays record 376 complete presented lifetimes across 50,083 observations.
Close train views show all five sprites, and paused screenshots match with tracing on
or off. The initial lifetime-audit failure remains preserved alongside its source-proven
pool-order correction.
