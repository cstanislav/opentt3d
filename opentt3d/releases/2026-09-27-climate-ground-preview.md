# Independent industry grounds across climates

Four provisional ground volumes bring the catalogue to **1,689 models** while
preserving all 1,685 preceding model hashes.

## Play

The [verified `.22` desktop release](https://github.com/cstanislav/opentt3d/releases/tag/opentt3d-dev-20260927.22)
is available for Windows, macOS and Linux. This climate-ground preview's executable
assets appear after its exact-commit package workflow passes. Required graphics are
bundled; see the [installation and first-launch guide](https://github.com/cstanislav/opentt3d/blob/main/opentt3d/PLAYING.md).

## New artwork and original behavior

- Independently painted Arctic/tropical soil and separate Arctic/tropical oil-well
  grounds, including paired foot strips and physically recessed, sealed well mouths.
- Toyland blue-grey paving with eight chamfered panels, connecting bands and grass.
- 336 explicit climate states for original ground sprites 3924/2173, plus 144
  verified Toyland bare-soil aliases. Machinery and ground retain independent owners.
- Complete playable footprints, source-compatible colours and supported quarter-unit
  cells preserve original construction, animation, cargo and simulation behavior.
- Ordinary coastal refinery fixtures preflight legal public construction commands
  before funding their oil producer.

## Still in progress

This is breadth-first, provisional artwork. Soil grain, oil rim/strip shapes, paving
details, registration and catalogue-wide fidelity remain open; final visual approvals
remain zero. Service controls average approximately 60fps but retain frame intervals
above 20ms. Sustained smooth 60fps across arbitrary worlds remains unfinished.
