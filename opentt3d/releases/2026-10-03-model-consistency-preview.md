# Road-stop, canal and bank consistency

The development increment repairs the reported road-stop ground mismatch,
flat canal walls and off-centre bank cupolas without changing simulation,
commands, saves or source-layer ownership.

- Bus/truck stops use their original full-tile ground chart and palette, at the
  same native texture scale as ordinary roads. Repeated tiny crops and duplicate
  procedural road markings are removed.
- Both bank cupolas are centred and partitioned across their original tile owners;
  shared roofs retain every cell without duplicate seam geometry.
- Twelve genuine stepped canal dikes follow the original side/convex/concave
  selections, including Classic's dynamic sprite IDs. Original climate-soil pixels
  and animated water remain independently owned. Custom/incomplete families and
  alternate graphics sets retain their complete original rendering.
- All12 live dike variants are observed in four climates on Vulkan and OpenGL.
  Ordinary test ships still visit both docks. Native210/asset174/harness74 and the
  140-file presentation-boundary check pass.
